Services

Flexible engagement models — project-based, retainer, or advisory — tailored to your needs. Below are the core areas of expertise.

DevOps & CI/CD

Build reliable, repeatable deployment processes with automated pipelines, GitOps workflows, and secure release strategies (blue/green, canary). Help teams ship faster with confidence.

What I deliver

  • CI/CD pipeline design and implementation (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)
  • Pipeline security, secrets management, and compliance
  • Infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible) and configuration management
  • Developer experience improvements and automation tooling

Cloud Engineering

Cloud-native architecture, repeatable IaC, migrations, and cost optimization across AWS, Azure, and GCP. Scalable, multi-region designs with containerized workloads.

What I deliver

  • Architecture reviews, reference designs, and migration planning
  • Infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Pulumi)
  • Containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Cost optimization, observability, and operational runbooks

Cybersecurity

Security assessments, threat modeling, secure SDLC guidance, and incident response planning. Hands-on hardening and remediation to reduce risk and protect critical systems.

What I deliver

  • Security posture assessments and compliance reviews
  • Vulnerability triage, remediation guidance, and server hardening
  • Incident response planning and tabletop exercises
  • Secure-by-default design and documentation

Typical engagements

  • Infrastructure audits & roadmaps: Assess current state, identify gaps, and provide actionable recommendations.
  • Cloud migrations: Plan and execute migrations to AWS, Azure, or GCP with minimal downtime.
  • CI/CD implementation: Design and build automated pipelines from scratch or modernize existing ones.
  • Security assessments: Review infrastructure, applications, and processes for vulnerabilities and compliance.
  • Ongoing advisory: Monthly or quarterly retainer for strategic guidance and hands-on support.
